# Data access and privacy

## DHS data

The analysis used the Nigeria 2023–24 DHS and Burkina Faso 2021 DHS under approved DHS project 242701, “Compound Climate and Conflict Shocks and Disruptions in the Continuum of Maternal Healthcare in Nigeria and Burkina Faso.” DHS survey microdata and geographic data cannot be redistributed here. Researchers must request access directly from [The DHS Program](https://dhsprogram.com/data/available-datasets.cfm) and comply with its terms of use.

## External exposure data

ERA5-Land, CHIRPS, and UCDP GED inputs are not bundled. They should be obtained from their official providers under the applicable versions and licenses described in the locked SAP.

## Deliberately excluded material

This release contains no individual records, household or cluster identifiers, coordinates, displaced GPS points, row-level linked records, point-level climate vectors, event-level conflict records, authentication material, download URLs tied to an account, or local machine paths.

Only disclosure-controlled aggregate results and figures are included.
